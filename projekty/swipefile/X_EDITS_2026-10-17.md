## Common DNA (what all 5 do)
- **Something new every 1.5–2.5 s** (caption change, pop-in object, insert, punch-in). Never a static 4 s stretch.
- **Captions = 2 tiers**: small context line + 1 BIG accent word (different size / colour / font). Accent colour is ONE per edit
  (yellow glow, electric blue, lime, green money). Negative words get red.
- **Objects sit on the chest / lower-middle**, never on the face. They POP (scale 0.6→1.05→1 with blur) and leave fast.
- **Full-screen inserts** 0.6–2 s to break the talking head: black / cream / colour page with ONE object (icon + cursor click,
  3D object, cards adding up, kinetic word). Return to face on the next cut.
- **Real-UI mock-ups** sell credibility: IG profile card, notification/reminder card, search bar typing, Figma selection box,
  IG comment at the end, chat bubbles.
- **Counters & accumulation**: money ticker climbing, cards adding up into a grid (cost stacks), emoji pills stacking.
- **Transitions**: light leak / white flash / colour wipe with paper-cut shapes, motion-blur whip on kinetic text. Short (≤0.3 s).
- **3D glossy objects** (icons as app tiles, brain, cash, box, watch) instead of flat icons — they read as premium.
